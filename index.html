About

Meet Lexa

Hi, I'm Lexa. I'm a mom of 2 little ones, a wife to my awesome husband, a lover of Jesus, and a part-time home designer. But birth is where my passion truly lies.

I became a doula because I believe birth can be so much more than what most of us have been told. I believe that our bodies were divinely designed by God to birth with hope and confidence. Just as God's mercies are new each morning (Lamentations 3:22-24) so each morning comes with hope and new possibilities, for with God, all things are possible (Luke 1:37).

So many of us walk into birth expecting fear, pain, and confusion. But I believe birth is a new opportunity to experience the goodness of God in a deeper way.

The surrender that birth requires is mental, physical, and spiritual, and it initiates us into motherhood in a way that no other event can. I believe a birth experience is not just deeply transformational for the mom, but also for the baby. This monumental milestone should be attended with intentional care and preparation.

So, I decided to become the kind of doula I want my sisters and my friends to have, and the kind of doula that I want you to have! I want you to have support that is steady, hopeful, and peaceful. I want you to have support that meets you in intensity, and reminds you that the power of birth is a good thing, from God, that he placed within you, so that you can finally meet your baby.

You are more than capable of walking through the night, and into the sunrise.

And that is how Sunrise Birth Co. began!

Doula 101

What is a doula?

A doula is a non-clinical support person who provides physical, emotional, and spiritual support to pregnant and birthing mothers — and serves as an advocate for moms during their births.

As a doula, I'm extensively trained in the physiology of birth, and can help you minimize pain through optimal fetal positioning, relaxation, and targeted pain-relief strategies.

Backed by research

Studies show continuous doula support leads to:

  • ✦A reduced likelihood of cesarean sections
  • ✦Decreased need for pain-relief medications
  • ✦Shorter labor durations on average
  • ✦Higher satisfaction with the birth experience
